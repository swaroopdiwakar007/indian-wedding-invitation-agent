# Quality gates

**Floor (never ships)**: invented facts, placeholder left in final, broken script rendering, contrast under 4.5:1, horizontal scroll at 320px, remote images or scripts, mixed sacred symbols without request, scripture not supplied.

**Reflex (defaults a model falls into; overrule only on the record)**:
- Gold gradient on everything.
- The same mandala behind every card.
- Centered everything with equal weight, so nothing leads.
- Script font for all text, including addresses.
- A heading above every section.
- Emoji in event headings.

**Finish (never blocks)**: ornament alignment at 1x and 2x, widows in names, consistent date format, tabular numerals for times, a printable A5 stylesheet.

Review: after building, rasterise at 390px wide and read it cold. Ask: what does it say first, is the date findable in two seconds, would the elders of this family recognise their tradition?
