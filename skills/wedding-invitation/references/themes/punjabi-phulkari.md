# Punjabi Phulkari

**Fits:** Sikh Anand Karaj, Punjabi Hindu weddings
**Palette:** ground #FFF8EC, ink #2A1B3D, accent #D81B60 magenta, support #F57C00 orange, #2E7D32 green
**Motif family:** Phulkari geometric diamonds as a border, Ik Onkar only at top for Sikh cards
**Type:** Tiro Gurmukhi; Noto Serif Gurmukhi; Lora
**Layout idea:** Diamond-lattice border, bold names, events as colour-blocked bars

Notes: compose an original motif from this family. Do not trace an existing card. Verify contrast of ink on ground before export.
