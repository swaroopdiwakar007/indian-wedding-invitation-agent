# Bengali Alpona

**Fits:** Bengali Hindu weddings
**Palette:** ground #FFFFFF, ink #7A1010, accent #C0392B, support #E8D5B5 terracotta wash
**Motif family:** Alpona line drawing: lotus, fish, vine, drawn as a single white-on-red stroke
**Type:** Tiro Bangla; Noto Serif Bengali; Playfair Display
**Layout idea:** Red top band with alpona, white body, names in Bangla and English

Notes: compose an original motif from this family. Do not trace an existing card. Verify contrast of ink on ground before export.
