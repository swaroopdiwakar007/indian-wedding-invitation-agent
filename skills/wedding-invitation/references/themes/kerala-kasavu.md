# Kerala Kasavu

**Fits:** Malayali Hindu and Syrian Christian weddings (swap symbols per tradition)
**Palette:** ground #FBF6E6 kasavu cream, ink #2B2B2B, accent #C9A227 gold, support #2E5E3E green
**Motif family:** Kasavu border stripes, nilavilakku lamp (Hindu) or a simple cross (Christian)
**Type:** Noto Serif Malayalam; Cormorant Garamond
**Layout idea:** Cream card with a gold double-stripe border, lamp or cross centered above the names

Notes: compose an original motif from this family. Do not trace an existing card. Verify contrast of ink on ground before export.
