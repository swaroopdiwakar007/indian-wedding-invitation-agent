# Gujarati Bandhani

**Fits:** Gujarati and Marwari weddings, garba and raas events
**Palette:** ground #FFF3E0, ink #3E1F00, accent #C62828, support #FFB300 and #00796B
**Motif family:** Bandhani dot clusters, mirror-work circles, garba circle
**Type:** Noto Serif Gujarati; Cormorant Garamond
**Layout idea:** Dot-pattern header, circular event badges, playful but ordered

Notes: compose an original motif from this family. Do not trace an existing card. Verify contrast of ink on ground before export.
