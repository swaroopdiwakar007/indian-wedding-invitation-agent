# Copywriting

- Use the family's wording where given. Otherwise a plain, formal line: "Together with their families, A and B request the pleasure of your company at the wedding of their children."
- Keep each section under 40 words.
- Bilingual cards: same content in both languages, regional language first only if the family asks. Do not machine-translate names. Ask for spelling.
- Dates: "Saturday, 14 February 2027" plus the time with am/pm. Add the tithi or muhurat only if supplied.
- Never add a blessing, shloka, couplet or quote that was not supplied.
- RSVP: one contact and one deadline, both supplied.
- No exclamation marks unless the family's text has them.
