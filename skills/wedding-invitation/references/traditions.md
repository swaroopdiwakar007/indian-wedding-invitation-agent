# Traditions

Respect over decoration. When unsure, ask. Never place a sacred symbol where it could be stepped on, cropped, or used as a pattern fill.

## Hindu
- Common: Ganesha or Om at top, kalash, mango leaves, diya, marigold, paisley (kairi), lotus.
- Invocation line allowed: "Shree Ganeshaya Namah" (Devanagari or transliterated).
- Events: Mehndi, Haldi, Sangeet, Vivah, Reception, Pheras. Keep event names as the family uses them.
- Do not use a deity's image as a repeated background.

## Sikh
- Common: Ik Onkar, Khanda (use sparingly, top of card only), phulkari patterns, Punjabi in Gurmukhi.
- Ceremony: Anand Karaj, at a gurdwara. Ask for the family's preferred greeting.
- Gurbani only if supplied exactly.

## Muslim
- Common: calligraphic Bismillah at top (only if supplied or the family confirms), arabesque, jali, crescent used lightly, green, gold, deep teal.
- Events: Nikah, Walima, Mehndi, Dholki. Avoid depicting people or animals if the family prefers.
- Urdu uses Nastaliq, right to left. Set `dir="rtl"` on that block only.

## Christian
- Common: cross or dove, floral, restrained palette. Goan, Kerala Syrian and North-East traditions differ; ask.
- Scripture only if supplied exactly.

## Jain, Buddhist, Parsi
- Ask for the symbol the family uses. Do not substitute.
- Parsi: names often in Gujarati and English; ask for the preferred invocation.

## Interfaith
- Equal weight to each tradition's symbols and language. Ask both families. Prefer neutral florals and geometry plus one symbol per side.

## Regional visual languages (original interpretation only)
- Rajasthani: arches, jharokha frames, block-print repeats, mirror work, saffron, rani pink, gold.
- Tamil/South: temple gopuram silhouette, kolam geometry, silk border bands, jasmine, kumkum red, temple gold.
- Bengali: alpona line work, red-and-white, terracotta, lotus.
- Punjabi: phulkari geometry, bright magenta, orange, green.
- Gujarati: bandhani dots, mirror work, garba motifs.
- Maharashtrian: nath, paithani border, mogra.
- Kerala: kasavu cream and gold border, oil lamp, banana leaf green.
- Kashmiri: chinar leaf, paisley, tilla-work gold, deep blues.
- Minimal modern: one motif, generous space, single accent.

These are starting points. Compose; do not trace.
