# Rubric

Score each 0 to 2.

1. Facts: nothing invented, placeholders resolved or build blocked.
2. Tradition: one tradition, respectful symbols, no mixed icons unless asked.
3. Script: Indic text renders, `lang` correct, fonts loaded.
4. Legibility: 16px body at 390px, contrast at least 4.5:1, no horizontal scroll.
5. Originality: not a copy of any known template; one clear idea.
6. Hierarchy: names and date lead; findable in two seconds.
7. Self-contained: no remote assets except Google Fonts.
8. Export: PNG sharp, names uncut, under 5 MB.

Pass: 13 of 16 with no zero on 1, 2 or 7.
